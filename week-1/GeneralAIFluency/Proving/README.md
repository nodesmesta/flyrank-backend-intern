## Proof Statement

> I translate abstract product ideas into deployed, testable AI prototypes. My work is built for Product Managers at seed-stage startups (5-15 people) whose engineering teams are too stretched to explore new ideas — they have the vision, I have the capacity to execute it fast. Starting from a vague notion, I clarify scope through direct conversation, break it down into architecture decisions, then build and deploy a working MVP within five days. Not a mockup, not a slide deck, but real running software you and your team can interact with immediately. After testing one of my prototypes, you'll know in minutes whether the idea deserves deeper investment — because your job is to validate quickly, and mine is to make that possible.

## One-Line Why

> A CV only lists what I've built; it cannot prove how I collaborate with non-technical stakeholders to clarify, translate, and execute an abstract idea into deployed software — which is the core skill this portfolio exists to demonstrate.
