## Pricing Overview

Asset management software cost varies from free open-source tools to $150+ per user per month for enterprise platforms.

| Plan / Tier | Typical Price Range | Best For |
|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| Free / Open Source | $0 (self-hosted) | Small teams, basic hardware tracking |
| SMB (Starter) | $20–$80/user/month | Small businesses, ~500 assets |
| Mid-Market | $80–$150/user/month | Teams needing integrations, workflows, reporting |
| Enterprise | $150+/user/month or custom annual ($13,500–$50,000+/year) | Multi-site, compliance-heavy |

## Pricing Models

1. **Per-User** — Most common. Scales with headcount. Risk: expensive as team grows.
2. **Per-Asset** — Based on number of assets, not users. More predictable for large organizations.
3. **Tiered / Feature-Based** — Basic → Professional → Enterprise. Risk: needed features locked behind higher tiers.
4. **Flat Subscription** — Fixed fee regardless of users/assets. Simple but rare at enterprise level.
5. **On-Premise / Perpetual License** — One-time fee + annual maintenance. Being phased out for cloud SaaS.

## Hidden Costs to Watch For

- Feature gating (audit trails, advanced analytics behind paywall)
- Integration fees (custom API dev: $5k–$25k+)
- Data migration ($5k–$25k+ for complex environments)
- Storage overage
- Training and onboarding
- Contract auto-renewals

## Cloud vs On-Premise

- **Cloud (SaaS):** Low upfront, predictable monthly fee, deploy in days/weeks, vendor-managed
- **On-Premise:** High upfront ($15k–$100k+), lower long-term, requires internal IT

## Evaluation Framework (4 Steps)

1. **TCO over 36 months** — subscription + implementation + integrations + training
2. **Scale simulation** — model cost at 2x and 5x current size
3. **Operational impact** — hours saved × labor cost
4. **Ecosystem fit** — integration depth to avoid fragmented data

## Key Takeaways

- Per-asset pricing tends to be more cost-effective for large enterprises
- Always request "total cost of ownership" over 3 years
- Cloud SaaS recoups upfront costs within 12–18 months through reduced IT overhead
- Most organizations underestimate implementation costs by 2–5x
