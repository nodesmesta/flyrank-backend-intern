## Key Thesis

Most IT asset tracking software tells you where a device *should be* — but doesn't ensure it *actually comes back*. The gap between tracking and physical recovery is where most tools fall short.

## What is IT Asset Tracking Software?

IT asset tracking software helps companies keep track of their devices — who has what, where it is, and whether it's still in use. Most tools do this by automatically detecting devices, logging them into a system, and updating their status over time.

**Categories tracked:**
- **Hardware assets:** Laptops, desktops, servers, mobile devices, monitors, printers, network equipment
- **Software assets:** Licensed applications, subscription services, cloud-based tools
- **Digital assets:** Certificates, configurations, access credentials
- **Network assets:** IP addresses, domain names, infrastructure components

## Key Features to Look For

1. **Automated Discovery and Tracking** — If your system depends on manual updates, your data will be wrong
2. **Integration Ecosystem** — Must sync with help desk, HR platform, identity provider, and finance tools
3. **Compliance and Security Management** — Access controls, audit history, visibility into asset activity
4. **Lifecycle Management** — Tracking through onboarding, usage, reassignment, and end-of-life
5. **Recovery (The Missing Piece)** — Returns, follow-ups, shipping, and confirmation of receipt require a separate process

## ITAM vs SAM vs HAM

- **IT Asset Tracking** — Monitoring location, status, ownership, and movement of technology assets
- **ITAM (IT Asset Management)** — Managing assets throughout entire lifecycle: procurement → deployment → maintenance → recovery → redeployment → disposal
- **HAM (Hardware Asset Management)** — Physical devices: inventory, device health, warranties, repairs, refresh cycles
- **SAM (Software Asset Management)** — Software licenses, subscriptions, installations, usage
- **Asset Discovery** — Automatically identifying devices connected to the network
- **Device Provisioning** — Preparing technology assets for employee use
- **Compliance Tracking** — Maintaining accurate records and audit trails
