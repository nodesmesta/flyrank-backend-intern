## Market Context

In 2026, organizations are dealing with growing asset volumes, remote teams, tightening compliance rules, and rising operational costs. Modern companies no longer rely on spreadsheets — they rely on intelligent asset management platforms that automate tracking, reporting, depreciation, lifecycle management, and service operations.

## Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) Defined

EAM software manages the entire lifecycle of physical assets — from procurement and usage to maintenance and disposal.

**Core capabilities:**
- Asset tracking & classification
- Preventive & predictive maintenance
- Compliance & audit readiness
- Depreciation & financial reporting

**2026 Trends in EAM:**
- AI integration
- IoT sensors
- Automation
- Cloud support
