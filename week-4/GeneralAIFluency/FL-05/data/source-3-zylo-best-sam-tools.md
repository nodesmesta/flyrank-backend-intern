On-premises software and SaaS create a complex environment for modern enterprise SAM teams to manage. In 2026, AI-native applications and consumption-based billing will make software asset management more important than ever to stay ahead of volatile costs and ensure compliance.

## Key statistics cited (2026 SaaS Management Index, Zylo)

- IT is responsible for just **15%** of software spend; lines of business and individuals account for the remaining **85%**.
- Organizations only use **57%** of their SaaS licenses on average → average **$80.6M** in wasted SaaS spend annually per organization.
- **78%** of IT leaders experienced unexpected charges on a SaaS bill due to consumption-based or AI pricing models.
- **393%** jump in AI-native application spend.

## What is a SAM tool?

A traditional SAM tool helps teams manage licenses, create effective license positions, and ensure audit readiness for on-premises software. A SaaS-forward tool does the same for SaaS with robust discovery. Core use cases: license management, a centralized system of record, spend optimization, renewal management, shadow IT detection, user-lifecycle automation, and integration with procurement/security/ITSM systems.

## Key features to evaluate

1. Software discovery and inventory (agent-based and agentless scanning; SSO/browser/expense/API connections for SaaS)
2. License management and reconciliation (entitlements vs consumption, compliance gaps)
3. Usage analytics and optimization (last login, feature-level engagement, adoption trends)
4. Workflow automation and integrations (HR, ITSM, procurement, ERP)
