# Survive the Crit

**Why it matters:**
You cannot see your own portfolio clearly after staring at it for weeks. A real second pair of eyes finds the confusion you've gone blind to, including the worst one, "I couldn't tell what you do." Taking that feedback well, without defending, is itself a professional skill, and it's the gate before you add power.

**Brief:**
1. Submit your live portfolio for design review (to a fellow student or a friend). Provide the proof statement from Chapter 1 so the reviewer can judge it against its actual job..
2. Ask the reviewer two questions first: in ten seconds, what do I do, and would you believe I'm good at it? Then collect the rest of the feedback without defending . Sort it: must-fix (confusing, broken, hurts the one action, the proof doesn't land) vs. nice-to-have (later).
3. Fix the must-fixes and reply with what you changed.

**Deliverable:**
The reviewer's feedback, your must-fix/nice-to-have sort, and evidence of the must-fixes addressed on the live site. **This checkpoint must pass to proceed to Week 7.**

**Pass / revise:**
- The portfolio was submitted with its proof statement, and real feedback was received.
- The reviewer could state what the member does and felt the work backed it up, or the gaps are now fixed.
- Feedback is sorted honestly into must-fix vs. nice-to-have.
- The must-fixes are actually fixed on the live site, not just acknowledged.
- The member engaged with the feedback rather than defending the original.
