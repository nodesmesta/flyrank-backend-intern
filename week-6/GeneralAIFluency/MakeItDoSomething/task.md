# Make It Do Something

**Why it matters:**
A static portfolio tells; a portfolio with one real feature does. Wiring exactly one thing, and understanding it, is the difference between a poster and a tool, and it's the most directly employable skill in the whole track.

**Brief:**
1. Choose the **one** dynamic feature your portfolio actually needs, most often a working contact form or email capture; for some, a live demo of your own work or a small AI feature. One, not several.
2. Wire it end to end on a free tier , with AI as your build partner. It has to genuinely work, a real submission reaches you, or the demo really runs.
3. Write a short plain-words explainer: what a backend is, what your feature does, and how the data flows, in your own words.

**Deliverable:**
The live feature working (evidence: a real test submission that reached you / the working demo), plus the plain-words explainer.

**Pass / revise:**
- Exactly one feature, working live end to end, not several half-wired.
- It's on a free tier and genuinely functions on a real test.
- The explainer is correct, in their own words, and shows they understand the data flow.
