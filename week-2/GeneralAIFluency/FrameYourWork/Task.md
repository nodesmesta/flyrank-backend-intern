# Frame Your Work

**Why it matters:** A great idea without a frame is invisible. This assignment is about using AI to interview yourself until the real story emerges — then framing it so a stranger immediately understands the problem, what you built, and why it matters.

**Brief:**
1. Define a **Voice Card** — 5 words that define your portfolio tone.
2. Run a structured AI **interview** around your project idea (Asset Guard). Let Claude ask one question at a time; answer honestly, even if nothing is built yet.
3. Draft a **Bio & Landing Page** — a prompt that generates orchestrated narrative copy with headline, hero, how-it-works, bio, and CTA — all in your voice card style.
4. Compare a **generic vs. edited version** of your landing page. Analyze what changed and why.
5. Write an **Implementation Plan** — break the idea into actionable build phases.

**Deliverable:** A README documenting the framing process with links to each artifact in `data/`.

**Evaluation criteria (pass/revise):**
- Voice card is distinct and used consistently across artifacts
- Interview captures honest answers (including what's NOT built yet)
- Bio and landing page demonstrate orchestration, not just description
- Generic vs. edited comparison shows clear improvement rationale
- Implementation plan is concrete and specific to Asset Guard
