Design a landing page for my portfolio project **Asset Guard**, with full orchestration — meaning the page should guide a visitor through a deliberate narrative flow from first impression to action.

**Voice card:** Direct, technical, deliverable, visionary, original. Write like a person, not a brochure.

**The problem it solves:** People and organizations own stuck assets — property, equipment, IP, inventory — that sit idle without generating value. No simple tool exists to check: "is this asset productive or not?"

**The product:** Asset Guard is a SaaS platform. Users upload a PDF of their asset, get a productivity score and specific improvement steps. Automated jobs then scrape live data (using the asset name as a keyword) to continuously monitor for new opportunities or risks.

**What I built:** I designed the architecture and flow end-to-end — minimal input friction, transparent scoring, automated monitoring that runs without user intervention.

**Target audience:** Product Managers at seed-stage startups who need to validate ideas fast.

**One action:** Email me to talk.

**Deliverables I need from you:**

1. **A headline and subheadline** — that states the problem and solution in one breath
2. **A hero section** — a short, plain description of what Asset Guard does
3. **How it works section** — 3-4 steps, short
4. **Two-sentence bio** — focused on Asset Guard, who I build for, not adjectives
5. **CTA line**

Orchestrate these so a visitor moves: sees the problem → understands the solution → believes I built it → takes action. No buzzwords. Keep it tight.
