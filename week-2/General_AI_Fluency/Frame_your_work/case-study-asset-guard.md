# Asset Guard — Case Study

## Voice Card
> Direct, technical, deliverable, visionary, original.

---

## The Problem

People and organizations own stuck assets — property, equipment, IP, inventory — that sit idle without generating value. No simple tool exists to check: "is this asset productive or not?"

## What I Did

I designed **Asset Guard**, a SaaS platform that detects and optimizes asset productivity.

How it works:
1. User signs up and uploads asset documents (PDF)
2. The system reads, analyzes, and assigns a **productivity score**
3. It provides **specific improvement steps** to make the asset more productive
4. Automated jobs continuously **scrape live data** — using the asset name as a keyword — to detect new opportunities or risks

Key design decisions:
- Minimal input friction: upload and go, no lengthy forms
- Transparent scoring: users see *why* a score is what it is
- Automated monitoring runs without requiring users to stay logged in

## What Came Of It

The problem is validated through direct experience with stuck assets. The architecture and flow are designed. Build is the next step.

## What I'd Do Differently

Start with a manual version first — scoring 5-10 real assets by hand — to validate the scoring model before writing any code.
