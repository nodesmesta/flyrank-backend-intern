# Consistency, Not Talent (and Frame, Not Upstage)

## Details
The skill this week is judgment, telling good from bad and choosing well, which is one of the most underrated parts of working with AI, and you'll practice it on your portfolio's look. First, the few simple choices that make any site feel intentional instead of amateur (no design talent required). Then the rule that's special to a portfolio: the design frames the work, it never upstages it. Then AI images: it can generate a hundred in minutes, and the real skill isn't making them, it's deciding which one serves your proof, rejecting the rest, and knowing when a real screenshot of your work beats anything generated. Learn to judge AI's output here and you'll judge it everywhere. You'll leave with a simple visual identity and images that belong together.
