# The Through-Line: Map Content & CTAs

**Why it matters:**
A good case in the wrong place still fails. Before you build, know exactly what goes on which page, the one-line claim that greets a visitor, and where every page sends them. This map is the bridge from words to a working portfolio.

**Brief:**
1. Write your **one-line claim** , the single sentence a visitor should remember, stating what you prove. (Use AI for ten options, then pick and sharpen one, the choosing is yours.)
2. Build a content map: for each page, list the sections in order, decide which cases sit where (lead with your strongest), and name the **call to action** , all laddering up to the one action from Week 1.
3. Note any proof you still need to gather (screenshots, a live demo link, the repo, before/after numbers, a testimonial), including internship work that isn't finished yet.

**Deliverable:**
he one-line claim + the content map (pages → sections → which case → call to action) + the "still need to gather" list, posted to your track thread.

**Pass / revise:**
- A single, memorable claim, not a paragraph.
- Every page has ordered sections and a named call to action; the strongest work leads.
- The calls to action ladder up to the one action from Chapter 1.
- The gather-list is honest, so the build week isn't blocked.
